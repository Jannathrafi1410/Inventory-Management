# Limitations

**Note:** All numbers in this repository are simulator outputs under stated assumptions, not TBO real P&L.

## Data and ground truth
- **Synthetic/simulated data:** history, demand, costs, cancellations, and scenarios are generated by the in-repo simulator (sim/*, config/assumptions.yaml), not observed from TBO production systems.
- **Latent demand is simulated:** data/ground_truth.parquet is synthetic and used only in /eval for realized/perfect-foresight evaluation; forecast/optimizer code never reads it by design.
- **Censoring model simplified:** observed sales are modeled as min(D, capacity) with simplified availability/booking dynamics; real marketplace censoring, partial availability, and multi-inventory interactions are more complex.

## Modeling and parameter assumptions
- **Assumed economics/parameters:** sell prices, dynamic/pre-buy costs, fallback availability a, goodwill g, capacities, contract terms (k,r), elasticities, pass-through, carry (12%/yr, 120d default), and risk settings are configured assumptions, not calibrated to TBO’s live economics.
- **Demand/scenario generation:** lognormal/correlated group-shock structure, intra-group correlation, volatility/correlation stress transforms, and forecast-to-scenario mapping are modeling choices that may not match real demand tails or regime shifts.
- **Unconstraining/calibration:** EM/unconstraining and conformal/quantile calibration are approximations on censored history; interval coverage targets are evaluated under documented split assumptions and may vary by slice.

## Product/market dynamics simplified
- **Cancellations simplified:** event/mixed cancellations and stress cases use simplified multiplicative scaling (e.g. event→35%, mixed→85%) and lead-time rules; real refund penalties, no-show behavior, rebooking, and supplier policy nuances are not fully modeled.
- **Pricing/execution simplified:** pricing grid, elasticity/pass-through, markdown ladder, tranching, priced WAIT, and release logic are heuristic/simulator policies; they do not capture full channel competition, negotiation outcomes, or operational constraints.
- **Supply constraints abstracted:** supplier caps, release deadlines, and concentration constraints are represented in simplified form; real allotment negotiations, overbooking rules, and contract legal terms are out of scope.

## Risk, optimization, and API
- **Risk/optimization approximations:** mean-CVaR LP may be solved on scenario subsets for tractability in some flows; constraints and reserve handling are prototype implementations.
- **Go API parity scope:** /api/v1/plan ports the water-filling allocator and reports plan metrics; beta/alpha are accepted but mean-CVaR LP allocation is not ported to Go (water-filling allocation is used), and min_fill_rate is advisory.
- **Performance:** demo re-solve is fast on demo-scale data, but large-scale benchmark 50k×1k (~6.18s on reference machine) does not meet the ~1s target.

## Evaluation and reproducibility scope
- **Sample/runtime tradeoffs:** some generated statistical artifacts may use reduced seeds/scenarios for practical runtime; spec-compliant scale-up commands are documented alongside results (e.g. docs/advanced_eval_results.md).
- **No live integration validation:** optional live TBO hotel search in UI is read-only with graceful fallback; end-to-end correctness against live TBO inventory/pricing/booking systems is not validated.
- **UI testing gap:** no headless browser E2E for Streamlit UI; verification relies on compile/targeted checks and API/allocator tests.
- **Pilot readiness:** results support a simulated prototype and analysis; a real 90-day pilot requires production data feeds, calibrated parameters, contract integration, monitoring/guardrails, and governance review.
